**Devoted Health's Arizona plans rate 3.5 to 4.5 stars for 2027, depending on which of its three contracts a plan sits on, and every Devoted plan with drug coverage in Maricopa County has a $0 monthly premium.** CMS's 2027 Star Ratings put Devoted's main Arizona HMO contract at 4 stars, its PPO contract at 3.5, and the contract carrying its new Arizona plans at 4.5. Those numbers say the carrier performs in the middle-to-upper range of this market on CMS's measures. They do not say whether a Devoted plan is right for you; that depends on whether your doctors, your hospital and your prescriptions are in the specific plan, and on how much of the in-network cap you could realistically spend.

What makes Devoted worth a post of its own this fall is direction. In a year when Aetna, Wellcare and others are shrinking their Arizona line-ups, Devoted is the one carrier nearly doubling its: 36 plans statewide for 2027 against 20 in 2026, and 16 in Maricopa County against 10. In north Peoria that means a lot of households are seeing the name for the first time on a comparison sheet, and a fair number of existing members are getting a 2027 notice with real changes in it.

## How big Devoted is in Arizona for 2027

According to CMS's 2027 plan landscape files, Devoted sells Medicare Advantage in the same ten of Arizona's fifteen counties it covered this year. The growth is in depth, not reach: sixteen new plans statewide, all of them on one contract, H7993, plus two Pima County plans that end. In Maricopa County, counting plans open to anyone with Medicare and leaving out chronic-condition plans, Devoted now has 10 of the county's 52 general plans, second only to Humana's 13. In 2026 it had 7, behind Humana and Aetna.

By September 2026, about 8,350 Maricopa residents were enrolled in a Devoted plan, according to CMS's monthly enrollment file. The largest single plan was Devoted Giveback 019, with 2,279 members, which matters for the section on what is changing.

A few things stand out once the plans are lined up.

**The same brand, three report cards.** Devoted's Arizona HMOs are split across two contracts. The four plans members already know (Core 001, 020, 021 and Giveback 019) sit on H8173, which CMS rated 4 stars for 2027, up from 3.5. The three new general HMOs sit on H7993, rated 4.5 overall, but that contract's name in CMS's files is Devoted Health Plan of Texas, and its rating was earned on members in other states before it sold anything here. Its drug-plan summary is 3 stars, lower than the 4 on the older Arizona contract. The PPO contract, H6586, rose from 3 to 3.5. If a sales sheet says "4.5-star Devoted plan," it is describing H7993 and only H7993.

**Most drug deductibles went down, not up.** Across the Maricopa market the standard Part D deductible rises from $615 this year to {{partD.maxDeductible}} for {{figYear}}, a figure CMS set in April, and [many Advantage plans are adding or raising a drug deductible](/blog/does-medicare-advantage-have-a-drug-deductible-2027/). Three existing Devoted HMOs moved the other way, from roughly $600 to roughly $460. Core 001 is the exception, going from $430 to $650.

**Two chronic-condition plans dropped their premium.** Devoted C-SNP 014 and C-SNP 022 each charged $17 a month in 2026 and are $0 for 2027. Those plans are only open to people with a qualifying condition such as diabetes or heart failure; [what a C-SNP is](/blog/what-is-a-c-snp-medicare-plan-arizona-2027/) explains who qualifies.

## If you already have Devoted Giveback 019

This is the plan to read your notice closely on, because it has the most Devoted members in the county and the biggest change. Its in-network cap rises by $1,000, to $8,900, one of the highest caps on any general plan in Maricopa for 2027. Its drug deductible falls by $144, which helps in January and does nothing in a year with a hospital stay.

For a healthy household that took the plan for the Part B reduction, that trade may be fine. For anyone who expects surgery, a cancer workup or regular specialist visits next year, an $8,900 ceiling is a number worth putting against your actual bills. Pull last year's statements and ask how close you came to $7,900, then add $1,000. [Whether a giveback is worth it at all](/blog/is-the-medicare-part-b-giveback-worth-it-peoria-az/) walks through that arithmetic, and [Maricopa's 2027 caps plan by plan](/blog/medicare-advantage-out-of-pocket-maximum-peoria-az-2027/) shows where Devoted sits against the rest of the county. Devoted Core 021, on the same contract, carries a $2,950 cap at $0, so a switch inside the carrier is possible if the network fits.

Your plan is continuing, so you received an Annual Notice of Change in late September rather than a non-renewal letter. [How to read that notice](/blog/medicare-annual-notice-of-change-letter-2027/) is the walk-through. You can switch between now and December 7 for a January 1 start, or stay where you are and do nothing.

## Four checks before calling any Devoted plan "good"

I hear a version of "my neighbor in Vistancia has Devoted and loves it" most weeks. A neighbor's experience tells you how a plan treated that person's doctors and that person's prescriptions. Here is the order I check things for a Peoria household instead.

1. **Your doctors, plan by plan.** Search the 2027 directory under the exact plan name, then call the office and ask whether they will be in network with that plan on January 1. Because Devoted's Arizona HMOs now run on two contracts, a cardiologist listed for Core 001 is not proof the same doctor takes Core 090. North Peoria specialist care leans on Sun City and the Arrowhead area, so start with those names. [How Advantage networks work](/learn/medicare-advantage-networks/) covers why the hospital being in network is not the same as every doctor inside it being in network.
2. **Your prescriptions on the 2027 formulary.** Enter your actual drug list in Medicare's Plan Finder for each Devoted plan you are considering. Tiers and pharmacy pricing move every January, and a drug deductible of $461 versus $700 is only meaningful once you know which tier your drugs land on.
3. **The cap against a bad year, not an average one.** Devoted's Maricopa caps run from $2,900 to $8,900 on the general plans. Pick the plan you could afford to hit the ceiling on.
4. **Your ZIP.** Quotes pull by ZIP code, and on the Peoria–Glendale–Sun City seam the line-up can differ between neighbors; [why Peoria plan lists differ by ZIP](/blog/medicare-plans-by-zip-code-peoria-az/) explains it. Check the line-up for your own ZIP, not a neighbor's.

The star rating belongs on that list too, as a tiebreaker rather than a starting point. [Which Arizona plans scored what for 2027](/blog/are-there-any-5-star-medicare-advantage-plans-in-arizona-2027/) has every Maricopa contract side by side.

## When Devoted, or any Advantage plan, is the wrong question

Some Peoria households are better served asking whether they want Medicare Advantage at all. The snowbird with a summer place in Prescott or out of state, the recent arrival whose specialists are scattered between Sun City, Scottsdale and a doctor back home, and the household that would rather pay a known premium than carry a cap usually end up comparing Original Medicare plus a Medicare Supplement against any Advantage plan, not one carrier against another. [Medicare Advantage against Medigap](/learn/medicare-advantage-vs-medigap/) lays out the trade.

The catch in Arizona is underwriting. There is no birthday rule here, and no Devoted plan in Maricopa is being discontinued, so a Devoted member who wants a supplement next year will usually answer health questions. People turning 65, and people in their first year of Advantage after turning 65, have protected windows; [switching from Advantage to Medigap in Arizona](/blog/can-i-switch-from-medicare-advantage-to-medigap-in-arizona/) covers who has a way back and who does not. If you are on a PPO for the travel flexibility, [the 2027 PPO options in Peoria](/blog/are-there-ppo-medicare-advantage-plans-in-peoria-az-2027/) compares Devoted Choice with the others.

## The dates that matter

- **Now through December 7, 2026:** Annual Enrollment. Switch to, from or between Devoted plans for a January 1, 2027 start.
- **January 1, 2027:** the new networks, formularies, deductibles and caps take effect.
- **January 1 through March 31, 2027:** Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment. If you are in an Advantage plan on January 1, you can make one change to another Advantage plan or to Original Medicare.

Your Part B premium of {{partB.premium}} a month in {{figYear}} continues whichever plan you pick; a $0 plan premium sits on top of it, not instead of it.
