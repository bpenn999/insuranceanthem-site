**Medicare's Annual Enrollment Period opened this morning, Thursday, October 15, 2026, and it closes Monday, December 7. Whatever you change takes effect Friday, January 1, 2027.** The window is the same across the country, so Peoria, Sun City West and Prescott all run on these dates. If you got a letter this month saying your Medicare Advantage plan is ending, this is the first day you can fix it. If you didn't, your job this week is probably just to confirm you have no reason to move.

That second group is most people. I'll start with the first group, because in the Valley it is larger this year than usual.

## Today is the first day to act if your plan is ending

The non-renewal letters are dated October 2, which is the federal 90-day notice. A new **Aetna Medicare Prime** plan does exist for 2027 under a different plan number. A matching name doesn't tell you whether you've been moved into it, and only your letter does. Don't assume either way.

Up in Yavapai County the shelf got thinner: five carriers this year, three next year, after Aetna and Wellcare left. If you spend part of the year in Prescott, read [the Prescott and Flagstaff plan post](/blog/medicare-advantage-plans-prescott-flagstaff-az-2027/) before anything else. The national picture explains part of this. Humana said on its July earnings call that about 600,000 members are in plans it is exiting for 2027, and other large carriers have trimmed service areas too. Your plan ending isn't a judgment on you, and you aren't losing Medicare. You're choosing a new way to receive it, and as of today you can.

## Which letter is on your kitchen counter?

Two different envelopes came this fall, and they ask for different things.

- **Annual Notice of Change** — sent to every Medicare Advantage and Part D member by September 30. Your plan continues, and the letter lists what changes on January 1. If nothing you use changed, you may be done. [How to read it in twenty minutes](/blog/medicare-annual-notice-of-change-letter-2027/).
- **Non-renewal notice** — dated October 2, and only if your plan is ending. If you do nothing, you return to Original Medicare on January 1. If your plan included drug coverage, you'll have none. [What to do if your plan is being discontinued](/blog/medicare-advantage-plan-discontinued-what-to-do-peoria-az-2027/) walks through all three options.

If you can't find either one, call the member-services number on the back of your card and ask which one you should have received. That call takes five minutes.

## Your checklist for this week

1. **Find the letter.** Put it on the table and work out which of the two it is.
2. **Write the drug list.** Every prescription with its dose, plus the pharmacy you actually use. The Part D cap rises to $2,400 for 2027, so a different formulary can still move your year by hundreds of dollars.
3. **Write the doctor list.** List your primary care doctor and every specialist you'd hate to lose by name, along with the practice and the hospital they use.
4. **Check those two lists against 2027, not 2026.** On the [Medicare plan finder](https://www.medicare.gov/plan-compare/), make sure the plan year at the top says 2027 and that you entered your own ZIP code.
5. **If your plan is ending, don't wait for December.** The plan you choose has to receive your enrollment by December 7 for a January 1 start. If you also want a Medicare Supplement, apply first. Your guaranteed-issue right opens around November 1.
6. **Put a decision date on the calendar before Thanksgiving.** The first week of December is the busiest week of the year in this business, and choices made then are usually rushed.

The [enrollment timeline tool](/tools/enrollment-timeline/) will tell you which window applies to you if your situation isn't the standard one. That includes turning 65 in the next few months, which runs on its own clock. [Turning 65 during open enrollment](/blog/turning-65-during-medicare-open-enrollment/) covers that case.

## The north Peoria check: is the network still where your doctors are?

Up the Lake Pleasant Parkway corridor, in 85383, Vistancia and Trilogy, the question is rarely whether a plan has a $0 premium. Almost all of them do. The question is whether the network still reaches the doctors you drive to. Most north Peoria households see specialists in the Arrowhead area or down in Sun City, and those practices don't all contract with the same plans.

That's also why your neighbor's answer may not be yours. On the [Peoria service-area page](/service-area/peoria-az/) I describe the Peoria–Glendale–Sun City seam, where two households a mile apart can be shopping different line-ups. If you live near it, look at the [Glendale page](/service-area/glendale-az/) too. Run your own ZIP, your own doctor list and your own drugs.

People who moved here from another state should check one more thing. Some arrived on a plan they bought back home, and some chose a PPO because it felt familiar. The Aetna Medicare Elite PPO ending this year has pushed a lot of those households to choose again. A PPO-to-PPO swap may work, but it isn't the only option this year.

## Advantage or a supplement? The window that matters for that question

Annual enrollment lets you leave Medicare Advantage for Original Medicare. It doesn't guarantee you a Medicare Supplement. Arizona has **no birthday rule**, so an ordinary supplement application can be underwritten and declined.

A discontinued plan changes that. It gives you a guaranteed-issue right to Plan A, B, D, G, K or L with no health questions. You can apply from 60 days before your coverage ends to 63 days after, which for a December 31 end works out to about **November 1, 2026 through March 4, 2027**. For a household in a forced switch, this may be the one opening in years to move to a supplement. [Switching from Advantage to Medigap in Arizona](/blog/can-i-switch-from-medicare-advantage-to-medigap-in-arizona/) and the [Plan G vs. Plan N tool](/tools/plan-g-vs-plan-n/) cover the trade-offs. Arizona has nine stand-alone drug plans for 2027 to pair with a supplement, and [the Part D price list](/blog/medicare-part-d-plans-arizona-2027/) shows how much each one rose.

## Star ratings, and what they cannot tell you

CMS released the 2027 Star Ratings on October 8. In Maricopa County four contracts rated 4.5 stars, none rated 5, and a couple of familiar names dropped. [The full table is here](/blog/are-there-any-5-star-medicare-advantage-plans-in-arizona-2027/). The ratings measure contract-level performance and member experience. They don't tell you whether your cardiologist is in network, so use them to break a tie, not to make the choice.
