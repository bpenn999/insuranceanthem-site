**Three carriers sell a general Medicare Advantage plan in Prescott and in Flagstaff for 2027 — Humana, UnitedHealthcare and Devoted Health — where there were five and six this year. Yavapai County has 16 general plans with drug coverage, Coconino has nine, and Mohave County, around Lake Havasu and Kingman, has nine from two carriers. Aetna and Wellcare no longer sell a general plan in any of the three, and about 5,100 people were in general plans that will not be sold where they live on January 1. Their Medicare is not ending. Their plan is, and they have three dates to work with.**

I am writing this from the Valley side of the hill, because that is where I get the question. A Peoria household in Vistancia or Blackstone has a place in Prescott or Munds Park and wants to know whether the plan works up there. A parent in Prescott Valley or Kingman has a letter dated October 2 and a son or daughter in 85383 trying to read it. And a fair number of north-country retirees come down I-17 for a specialist at Arrowhead or in Sun City. The Maricopa County list barely moved for 2027: 46 general plans became 45, from the same thirteen carriers. North of the county line it is a different story.

## What changed on the plans that stayed

Most people up north are in a plan that continues, so their mail is an Annual Notice of Change, not a non-renewal letter. Worth reading anyway:

- **AARP Medicare Advantage from UHC AZ-0010 (PPO)**, the largest general plan in Yavapai and Coconino with about 7,300 members between them, stays at $0. Its in-network cap rises from $6,700 to $7,150.
- **AARP Medicare Advantage from UHC AZ-0008 (PPO)** goes from $45 to $64 a month, and its cap from $5,900 to $7,150.
- **Humana Gold Plus H0028-024 (HMO)**, with more than 15,000 members in Mohave County, stays at $0 with a cap of $5,400, up from $4,900.
- **Humana Gold Plus H0028-062 (HMO)** in Yavapai stays at $0; the cap goes from $4,900 to $5,999.
- **HumanaChoice R7220-002 (Regional PPO)** goes from $37 to $110 a month.

The 2027 Star Ratings came out October 8. Humana's Gold Plus HMO contract and the contract carrying Devoted's three new HMOs are at 4.5 stars, the UnitedHealthcare and HumanaChoice PPO contracts are at 4, Devoted's PPO contract is at 3.5, and Humana's regional PPO is at 3. Ratings are given to a whole contract, often across several states, and they measure things like screenings and call-center performance. They do not tell you whether your cardiologist is in network. ## If your plan left: three doors, three dates

A plan that ends and a plan that stops serving your county give you the same rights.

**Another Medicare Advantage plan.** Annual Enrollment runs **October 15 to December 7** and is the only window with a clean January 1 start. After that, a special period for non-renewed plans runs **December 8 through February 28, 2027**, with coverage starting the first of the month after you enroll and Original Medicare in between. With two or three carriers on the menu, the comparison is short, and it should start with your doctors and the nearest hospital, not the premium.

**Original Medicare with a Medicare Supplement and a Part D plan.** From **November 1, 2026 through March 4, 2027** a supplement carrier must issue you Plan A, B, D, G, K or L with no health questions. Arizona has no birthday rule and no yearly Medigap window, so for anyone past their first year this is the exception, not the norm. In a county with two carriers and an hour's drive to a specialist, a card that works with any doctor who takes Medicare is worth pricing. [Plan G against Plan N](/blog/plan-g-vs-plan-n-arizona-which-should-i-choose/) is the next read, and every Part D plan carries the [{{nextYear}} out-of-pocket cap](/blog/medicare-part-d-out-of-pocket-cap-2027/).

**Doing nothing.** On January 1 you are in Original Medicare with no drug plan and no annual cap, and after 63 days without creditable drug coverage a permanent Part D penalty begins. It is only a plan if the supplement and drug plan are already arranged.

[What to do when your plan is discontinued](/blog/medicare-advantage-plan-discontinued-what-to-do-peoria-az-2027/) walks the calendar week by week.

## If you live in Peoria and summer up north

Your plan follows your permanent address, the one Social Security has on file. A cabin in Prescott or Flagstaff does not give you Yavapai's or Coconino's menu, and it never did, so nothing on the lists above ends your Maricopa plan. What changes is the reasoning some households used to choose it.

- **An HMO covers emergencies and urgently needed care anywhere.** Routine care is another matter. A Valley HMO's network is built around the Valley, and a follow-up visit in Prescott may not be covered at all.
- **A PPO pays out of network at a higher cost share**, with any provider willing to bill it. If you spend June through September at 7,000 feet, that is the feature you are buying, and it is worth checking what the out-of-network cap is, since it is separate from the in-network one and higher.
- **A carrier name is not a network.** If you chose Aetna or Wellcare partly because the same name was sold up the hill, it no longer is. Even where a carrier sells in both counties, the Maricopa plan and the Yavapai plan are different plans with different provider lists.
- **Six months is the line.** A plan must disenroll a member who is outside its service area for more than six consecutive months, unless it has a visitor or travel benefit that extends that. Summer people rarely get close. Households drifting toward living up north full time should notice when the cabin has quietly become the permanent address.

That last case cuts the other way too. If you move your permanent address from Prescott down to Peoria, the move itself opens a special enrollment period and you go from three carriers to thirteen. [Changing Medicare when you move](/blog/do-i-have-to-change-my-medicare-if-i-move-to-arizona/) covers the timing, and [using a plan away from home](/blog/can-i-use-my-medicare-advantage-plan-in-another-state/) covers the travel rules in full. There is also a local wrinkle: Peoria's city limits run north past Lake Pleasant into Yavapai County, and [which county your address is in](/blog/medicare-plans-by-zip-code-peoria-az/) decides which list you shop from.

If the months away are the real driver, it is the supplement door again. Original Medicare does not care which county you are standing in.

## The short version

- **Prescott and Flagstaff each have three Medicare Advantage carriers for 2027**: Humana, UnitedHealthcare and Devoted Health. Lake Havasu and Kingman have two.
- **Aetna and Wellcare no longer sell a general plan in Yavapai, Coconino or Mohave.** About 5,100 general-plan members are affected across the three.
- **In Coconino the lowest in-network cap goes from $3,000 to $6,050**, and the largest continuing PPO up north raises its cap to $7,150.
- **If your plan left, the Medigap door is open November 1 to March 4 with no health questions.** In Arizona there is no other routine way around underwriting.
- **A Peoria plan is still a Peoria plan in Prescott.** Emergencies are covered anywhere; routine care up north depends on whether you hold an HMO or a PPO.
