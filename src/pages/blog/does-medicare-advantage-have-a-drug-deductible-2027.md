**Yes, most Medicare Advantage plans have a drug deductible, and in 2027 fewer of them skip it. In Maricopa County, 42 of the 45 general Medicare Advantage plans with drug coverage charge one next year, according to CMS's 2027 plan landscape files. Only three are $0, down from six this year, and the federal ceiling rises from {{partD.maxDeductible}} to $700.**

I see the same thing every fall in Peoria kitchens. Someone reads the premium on their Annual Notice of Change, sees $0 again, and puts the letter down. The drug deductible sits a few lines lower. For 2027 it is the number that moved: 26 of the 38 plans renewing in the county raised it, and three plans that had no deductible at all now have one. That is real money in January and February, and it lands on exactly the people who take a brand-name drug.

## How a drug deductible works on a Medicare Advantage plan

A Medicare Advantage plan with drug coverage carries two separate sets of cost sharing. The medical side has its own copays and, sometimes, its own deductible. The drug side follows the Part D rules, and that is where this deductible lives.

Until you have paid the deductible amount in a calendar year, you pay the plan's full negotiated price for drugs the deductible applies to. After that, you pay the tier copay or coinsurance. Three details change how much it bites:

- **Most plans exempt the cheap tiers.** Every Maricopa plan with a 2027 drug deductible also lists at least one tier the deductible does not touch, typically preferred generics. If your list is all tier 1 and tier 2 generics, you may never pay a dollar of it.
- **Some drugs never face it.** Covered insulin is capped at $35 a month with no deductible, and CDC-recommended adult vaccines, including the shingles shot, cost nothing.
- **It counts toward the drug cap.** Whatever you pay during the deductible goes toward the $2,400 Part D out-of-pocket limit for 2027. It does not count toward the medical out-of-pocket maximum, which is a separate ceiling covered in [the 2027 Peoria out-of-pocket maximum post](/blog/medicare-advantage-out-of-pocket-maximum-peoria-az-2027/).

The deductible starts over every January 1, on every plan, whether you switch or stay. The Learn page on [why drug costs jump in January](/learn/why-drug-costs-jumped-in-january/) walks through the reset in more detail.

## The three plans that still have none, and the five that went down

For 2027 the only general Medicare Advantage plans in Maricopa County with a $0 drug deductible are SCAN Classic (HMO), SCAN MyChoice (HMO) and Alignment Health ONE + Walgreens 001 (HMO). All three held at $0 from this year.

Five renewing plans lowered theirs: HealthSpring Preferred Extra Savings (HMO) from $500 to $300, Wellpoint Medicare Advantage (HMO-POS) from $200 to $130, and three Devoted plans (Giveback 019, Core 020 and Core 021) from around $600 to about $460.

Among the plans that are new to the county for 2027, the deductible runs from $400 on Aetna Medicare Prime (HMO), H8332-008, to $700 on Humana Value Choice (PPO) and Devoted Giveback Extras 105.

Plan names here are reporting from the CMS files, not a recommendation, and every plan listed is an HMO or PPO with a network. A $0 drug deductible on a plan that does not include your Arrowhead specialist is not a bargain.

## When a $500 deductible matters, and when it does not

The deductible is easy to over-weight or to ignore entirely. In practice it comes down to what is on your prescription list.

**Generics only.** A Vistancia retiree on a statin, a blood-pressure pill and a thyroid tablet, all preferred generics, probably never touches the deductible on most of these plans. For that household the network, the medical copays and the out-of-pocket maximum matter far more.

**One brand-name drug.** Take a Westbrook Village couple where one spouse fills a brand-name inhaler or blood thinner every month. On a plan with a $685 or $700 deductible, the first month or two of that drug is billed at the plan's full price until the deductible is met. On a $200 plan, it is a few weeks. The difference between those plans can be close to $500 in the first quarter, every year, before any copay.

**Expensive drugs.** If your drugs will reach the $2,400 cap regardless, the deductible mostly changes *when* you pay rather than *how much*. It is part of the $2,400, not on top of it. The [Medicare Prescription Payment Plan](https://www.medicare.gov/prescription-payment-plan) lets you spread those costs over the year in monthly bills instead of paying them all in January.

A couple in Trilogy at Vistancia on the same plan has two deductibles, one each. They are not shared.

## The Medigap side of the same question

If you have a Medicare Supplement, your drug coverage is a stand-alone Part D plan, and the deductible question is the same with different plans. Every stand-alone drug plan in Arizona raised its premium for 2027, and the four $0-premium ones are gone; the plan-by-plan list is in [Part D plans in Arizona for 2027](/blog/medicare-part-d-plans-arizona-2027/).

Households that pay IRMAA should remember that the Part D income surcharge is billed on top of the plan, whichever plan it is. A lower deductible does not touch it.

## What to check before December 7

1. **Find the deductible on your Annual Notice of Change.** It is in the drug section, usually on the first two pages of the summary. If you did not keep the letter, [here is what it contains and where to find it](/blog/medicare-annual-notice-of-change-letter-2027/).
2. **Look at which tiers it applies to.** The same letter says whether tiers 1 and 2 are exempt. That one sentence decides whether the deductible means anything to you.
3. **Run your actual drug list** through Medicare's [Plan Finder](https://www.medicare.gov/plan-compare/) for your plan and two others. Plan Finder adds premium, deductible and copays into one yearly total, which is the number to compare.
4. **Check the network before the drug total.** The cheapest drug plan is the wrong plan if your doctors are out of network. For a north Peoria household that usually means Arrowhead, Banner Thunderbird or the Sun City specialists.
5. **Put a whole year on one line.** The site's [Medicare cost estimator](/tools/medicare-cost-estimator/) helps compare premiums, deductibles and worst cases side by side.

The fall enrollment window closes December 7, 2026, and changes take effect January 1, 2027, the same day the new deductible starts counting. If your plan is ending rather than changing, the dates and options are different; start with [Medicare Advantage plans ending in Arizona for 2027](/blog/medicare-advantage-plans-ending-arizona-2027/).

## The short version

- Most Medicare Advantage plans have a separate drug deductible. In Maricopa County that is 42 of 45 for 2027.
- The ceiling is $700 in 2027. Twelve local plans charge all of it.
- Only SCAN Classic, SCAN MyChoice and Alignment Health ONE + Walgreens 001 have none. Three Humana plans that were $0 now charge $200 to $450.
- Generics on exempt tiers, insulin and recommended vaccines skip the deductible.
- It counts toward the $2,400 drug cap, not the medical out-of-pocket maximum.
